Brain visualization assets

brain-cortex.glb: MRI-derived geometry from OpenNeuro ds006128, subject sub-01,
snapshot 1.0.11. Dataset and geometry: CC0 1.0 Universal.
Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds006128.v1.0.11
Upstream model: https://github.com/StarKnightt/brain-explorer/blob/master/public/models/brain-atlas.glb
Upstream provenance: https://github.com/StarKnightt/brain-explorer/blob/master/public/models/brain-atlas.provenance.json
Original SHA256: ce761741d866e32d7a5b7638f4cacd5ec03e2b582e72c8218aba483ac28125e8
Modification: retained unified cortex, cerebellum and brain stem, with positions,
normals and cortical curvature; removed unused region meshes and attributes.
Activation colors are a synthetic animation, not measured brain activity.
