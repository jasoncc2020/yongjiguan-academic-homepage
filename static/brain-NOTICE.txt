Schaefer 200 connectome illustration
Atlas: Schaefer2018_200Parcels_7Networks_order, FSLMNI152 1mm.
Coordinates: official parcel centroids in RAS millimetres from Thomas Yeo Lab / CBIG.
Source: https://github.com/ThomasYeoLab/CBIG/blob/master/stable_projects/brain_parcellation/Schaefer2018_LocalGlobal/Parcellations/MNI/Centroid_coordinates/Schaefer2018_200Parcels_7Networks_order_FSLMNI152_1mm.Centroid_RAS.csv
Paper: Schaefer et al. (2018), Local-Global Parcellation of the Human Cerebral Cortex from Intrinsic Functional Connectivity MRI. Cerebral Cortex 28(9):3095-3114. https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhx179
brain-schaefer200.json retains all 200 centroid coordinates and atlas labels.
The axial connectome projects the original R/A coordinates into a glass-brain outline.
Connections are resampled between time windows and interpolated continuously.
Node colors group the seven networks; edges and their fluctuations are generated
for illustration and do not represent measured functional connectivity.
brain-connectome-fallback.svg is generated from the same atlas coordinates.
